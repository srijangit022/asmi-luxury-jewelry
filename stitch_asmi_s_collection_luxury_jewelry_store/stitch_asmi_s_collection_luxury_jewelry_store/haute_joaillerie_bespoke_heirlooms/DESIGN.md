## Brand & Style

The design system establishes an ultra-luxury digital gallery experience celebrating handcrafted heirloom jewelry. It merges the gravitas of classical Indian goldsmithing and pearl curation with modern European high-fashion editorial aesthetics. The target audience comprises discerning patrons, private collectors, and bridal connoisseurs seeking museum-grade bespoke adornments.

The aesthetic philosophy embraces **Architectural High-Fashion Minimalism**:
- **Purity and Precision:** Vast negative space punctuated by razor-sharp rectangular geometry.
- **Editorial Contrast:** Razor-thin hairline frames, dramatic typographic hierarchy, and museum plinth framing that allows the luster of pearls, diamonds, and gold bullion to command complete focus.
- **Monastic Luxury:** Restrained ornamentation where luxury is conveyed not through gratuitous gradients or decorative noise, but through extreme typographic control, precise grid alignment, and disciplined rhythm.

## Colors

The palette balances monumental obsidian blacks, radiant gold leaf, royal gemstone accents, and stark gallery whites:

- **Primary Gold (`#D4AF37`):** The signature royal champagne gold, reserved for primary interactive triggers, active navigation markers, key certification badges, and delicate metallic dividers.
- **Secondary Obsidian (`#0A0A0A`):** Deep volcanic black utilized for dominant typography, structural bounding borders, high-impact solid CTAs, and dramatic product silhouette stages.
- **Tertiary Crimson (`#800020`):** Rich royal ruby red inspired by traditional Burmese pigeon-blood gems. Used strictly for rare limited editions, bespoke commission badges, sold-out statuses, and exclusive collection accents.
- **Neutral Alabaster (`#FAFAFA`):** An immaculate, museum-grade off-white canvas that eliminates eye fatigue while maintaining razor-sharp contrast against dark typography and luminous jewelry photography.
- **Supporting Metallic & Monochromes:**
  - Gold Luster (`#E6CA65`): Hover and illuminated states.
  - Antique Gold (`#C5A059`): Subtle secondary text, muted borders, and fine filigree accents.
  - Charcoal Surface (`#121212`): Full-bleed dark mode modal backdrops and dramatic presentation banners.
  - Alabaster Border (`#EAEAEA`): Hairline boundary division.

## Typography

The typographic system contrasts **Playfair Display** (haute-couture transitional serif conveying heritage, craft, and romance) with **Montserrat** (architectural geometric sans-serif engineered for clarity, diamond carat specifications, and navigational precision).

Rules of execution:
- Headings display high stroke contrast; titles and subheadings frequently employ delicate italics to evoke handwritten archival bespoke certificates.
- All category labels, meta attributes, breadcrumbs, pricing units, and button triggers utilize `label-caps` or `label-micro` with wide tracked spacing (`letter-spacing: 0.2em` to `0.25em`) and strict uppercase transformations.
- Body text maintains a light optical weight (`font-weight: 300`) with generous line-heights to ensure effortless legibility across editorial descriptions.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is grounded in a disciplined 12-column architectural grid inspired by physical art monograph folios:

- **Desktop (1440px and above):** 12 columns with 48px gutters and 96px outer canvas margins (`margin-lg`). Modules follow generous breathing space, with section separation set to `space-2xl` (96px).
- **Tablet (768px – 1439px):** 8 columns with 32px gutters and 48px margins (`margin`). Elements collapse into two-up presentation blocks.
- **Mobile (below 768px):** 4 columns with 16px gutters and 24px margins (`margin-sm`). Editorial blocks flow sequentially; product imagery maintains edge-to-edge full bleed or rigid 1:1 square modules.
- **Curatorial Rhythm:** Layouts alternate between dense specification rows (split-table views) and expansive cinematic galleries with asymmetric balance, deliberately breaking symmetrical expectations to highlight rare individual showpieces.

## Elevation & Depth

In keeping with high jewelry salon design, artificial drop shadows are strictly prohibited. Depth is achieved via **low-contrast outlines, spatial layering, and crisp tone shifts**:

- **Haute Borders & Frames:** Elevation is defined by crisp, 1px hairline dividers (`#EAEAEA` on light surfaces; `#262626` on obsidian surfaces). Fine gold hairlines (`#D4AF37`) demarcate active selections and VIP tier layers.
- **Tonal Plinths:** Layers do not float; they sit on structured planes. Modals, drawers, and flyouts manifest as pure Alabaster White (`#FFFFFF`) or pure Obsidian (`#0A0A0A`) sheets with full-perimeter 1px boundaries.
- **Backdrop Veil:** Overlays for private viewings or image zooms employ an ultra-dense `#0A0A0A` backdrop with 85% opacity and no blur, evoking the dramatic light cutoff of an exclusive salon viewing room.

## Shapes

The shape system is uncompromisingly **Sharp (`roundedness: 0`)**.

- Every button, card, image frame, input container, modal dialog, and badge features a pure 0px corner radius.
- This architectural geometry mirrors the step-cut facets of emeralds, baguette diamonds, and gold ingots, reinforcing structural discipline, unyielding craftsmanship, and high-fashion authority.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Triggers
- **Primary Action (Acquisition / Inquire):** Solid `#0A0A0A` background with crisp `#FFFFFF` uppercase typography (`label-caps`). 0px border radius. On hover, background shifts smoothly to `#D4AF37` with `#0A0A0A` text.
- **Secondary Action (Add to Vault / View Details):** Transparent background with a 1px solid `#0A0A0A` border. Text in `#0A0A0A`. Hover inverts to solid `#0A0A0A` with `#FFFFFF` text.
- **Bespoke VIP Trigger:** Solid `#D4AF37` gold surface with `#0A0A0A` bold tracking. Hover transitions to `#C5A059`.
- **Text Action:** Naked label with an animated 1px underline anchored 4px below the text, expanding from center on hover.

### Product Presentation Cards
- Cards feature strict rectilinear containers with a 1px `#EAEAEA` perimeter line.
- Imagery is presented against a pure neutral background with generous internal padding (`space-lg`), highlighting jewelry details without background clutter.
- Metadata layout: Collection tier in `label-micro` (`#C5A059`), Piece Title in `headline-sm` Playfair Display, Gemstone / Metal specification in `body-sm` (`#666666`), and Price in `label-caps` (`#0A0A0A`).

### Input Fields & Selectors
- Flat input surfaces with bottom-only 1px borders in `#0A0A0A`. On active focus, the border transitions to `#D4AF37` with an upward-floating label in `label-micro`.
- Dropdown selectors utilize pure square flyout menus bordered by 1px hairlines, featuring hover row highlights in subtle off-white tones (`#F5F5F5`).

### Chips & Filter Tags
- Zero border radius rectangles.
- Inactive state: 1px `#EAEAEA` border, transparent surface, `#0A0A0A` text in `label-caps`.
- Active state: Solid `#0A0A0A` background with `#FFFFFF` text, or framed with a double hairline 1px `#D4AF37` border for precious gem categories.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- Radio controls are sharp diamond-rotated squares (`rotate(45deg)`) rather than circles, referencing faceted diamond shapes.
- Checkboxes are crisp 16x16px squares with 1px border. Checked state renders a solid `#0A0A0A` center with an antique gold checkmark.

### Bespoke Domain Components
- **Certificate / Provenance Drawer:** A dedicated full-height sliding split-panel detailing diamond grading, pearl luster indices, and hallmark verification via gold-ruled data tables.
- **Magnification Loupe Modal:** Fullscreen zoom stage offering 400% macro view of pavé settings and metalwork over pure Obsidian Black.
